## 1. Purpose

This document limits how a retention message may be sent. It is not a script that claims the model is certain.

## 2. Contact limits

- At most two outbound retention contacts in any 30-day window.
- This prototype does not store contact history. The employee must check the account record before sending. If the history cannot be checked, do not send.
- A Low-risk customer who is not flagged is not contacted under this policy.

## 3. Channels

- Start with email or the in-app message centre.
- A phone call is allowed only for a High-risk customer who is flagged, and only after the written message if a phone number is already on file.
- Do not use a channel the customer has opted out of. This prototype does not store marketing consent. If consent is unknown, do not contact; ask a manager how the account’s preference is recorded.

## 4. Prohibited tactics

- Do not invent a deadline (“this offer expires tonight”) unless the offer policy states that duration.
- Do not say the model proved the customer will leave.
- Do not say a SHAP factor caused the risk.
- Do not hide that a discount is temporary or quote a higher percent than the approved tier.
- Do not pressure a customer who has asked not to be contacted.

## 5. Example

Allowed. An employee emails a flagged month-to-month customer: “We can apply a 5% bill reduction for 3 months, then the current price resumes. A manager has not yet confirmed this month’s budget.”

Not allowed. “Our AI determined you are about to cancel, and this is your last chance to keep service.”
