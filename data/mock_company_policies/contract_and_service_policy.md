## 1. Purpose

This document states fictional contract limits that apply before a retention offer. The dataset fields used here are Contract, InternetService, and TechSupport. The dataset has no contract end date, so no rule depends on days remaining.

## 2. Contract changes

- A month-to-month customer may be invited to a one-year or two-year term. That invitation is not a discount. Any price reduction still has to match the retention offer policy.
- A one-year customer is not eligible for an employee discount. Only the manager exception in the offer policy can apply.
- A two-year customer cannot be given a retention discount under these documents.
- There is no early-termination fee in this fictional policy. Do not invent one.

## 3. Tech-support add-on

When a flagged Medium-risk or High-risk customer has InternetService of DSL or Fiber optic and TechSupport of No, an employee may offer one complimentary month of tech support.

This add-on is not a price discount. It does not use the 8 FCU authority limit. It is not offered together with Tier C, because Tier C already addresses fiber customers without tech support through a manager-approved discount.

The add-on is a proposal. This project has no outcome data showing that the add-on reduces churn.

## 4. Plan changes and escalations

- Moving a customer to a cheaper published plan is manager-only, and only when a current plan catalogue is available. This prototype has no plan catalogue, so the assistant must not recommend a specific cheaper plan.
- A technical fault that is not solved by the complimentary support month is handed to the support queue. It is not a reason to exceed the discount cap.

## 5. Examples

Allowed. Fiber optic, TechSupport No, Medium risk, flagged, tenure 2, month-to-month. No discount tier matches. The employee may offer the one-month tech-support add-on.

Not allowed. Two-year contract with a 10% “loyalty” discount created in the conversation. The offer policy does not contain that discount.
